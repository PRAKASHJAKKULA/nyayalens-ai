## 🌟 Executive Summary

**NyayaLens AI** is an explainable early-warning and decision-support platform that monitors public infrastructure development projects (12,480+ active MPLADS schemes worth ₹2,840 Cr). It identifies statistical expenditure anomalies, duplicate works, implementation delays, and contractor overload clusters, presenting mathematically defensible evidence to government auditors.

---

## 🏗️ 9 Core Intelligence & Security Modules

1. **🗺️ Hero Geospatial Risk Map & 3D Elevation Terrain**: Interactive India state/district drill-down + 3D WebGL risk pillars.
2. **🧠 Multi-Signal AI Risk Engine & Peer Intelligence**: Segmented IQR cohort cost comparisons + SHAP waterfall breakdown ($0–100$ scale).
3. **🔎 Project Case Files & Vertical Lifecycle Timeline**: Chronological milestones with automated progress stagnation and front-loading alerts.
4. **🕸️ Relationship Network Intelligence**: Canvas node-link mapping of Projects $\leftrightarrow$ Agencies $\leftrightarrow$ Contractors $\leftrightarrow$ Duplicate Overlaps.
5. **⏳ 3D Asset Digital Twin**: Three.js structural CAD visualizer exposing physical-vs-financial discrepancies.
6. **🤖 Grounded AI Investigator (RAG)**: Conversational command terminal citing *MoSPI MPLADS Guidelines 2023*.
7. **📱 Mobile Field Inspection App**: Geofenced GPS validation ($12\text{m}$ proximity check) + camera capture with SHA-256 digital proofing.
8. **🔐 Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Ledger & Review Vault**: Cryptographic digest chain recording every auditor determination.
9. **🛡️ 9-Domain Security & SIEM Operations Center (SOC)**: Certified against CERT-In, NIC IAM 2023, and ISO/IEC 27001 standards.
